    Common Red Flags

    Fake urgency

    “Act now or your account will be blocked.” Scammers rush you so you can’t think.

    Suspicious links

    Shortened or look-alike links that imitate your bank or a courier service.

    Requests for secrets

    No genuine company asks for your OTP, PIN, CVV or password.
